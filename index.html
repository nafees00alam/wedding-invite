Getting Here

Travel & Stay Information

Everything you need to plan your journey to Islampur

✈️

By Flight

Nearest Airport: Bagdogra Airport (IXB)
Distance: ~60 km
Travel time: 1.5 – 2 hours by car/taxi

🚆

By Train

Nearest Stations: Kishanganj (KNE) & Aluabari Road (AUB)
Well connected from Kolkata, Delhi & other major cities

🚗

By Road

Islampur is well connected via NH 31.
From Siliguri: ~45 km (1 hr)
From NJP/Bagdogra: ~60 km (1.5 hrs)
